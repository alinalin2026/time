# TimeBucks Prelander — Design Philosophy

## Design Direction: "Empowered Opinions"

**Theme:** Modern, approachable, women-centric rewards platform
**Aesthetic:** Clean, contemporary design with purple accents, emphasizing trust and opportunity
**Emotional Intent:** Confident, inclusive, and rewarding—positioning women as valued contributors whose opinions matter

---

## Core Design Principles

1. **Trust Through Clarity** — No hidden earnings or ambiguous promises. Clear, honest messaging about rewards and qualification process.
2. **Women-First Visual Language** — Warm, inclusive imagery and typography that speaks directly to women's experiences and aspirations.
3. **Frictionless Interaction** — Minimal steps to qualify; emphasis on "See if you qualify" rather than hard sells.
4. **Purple as Empowerment** — Rich purple (#7C3AED or similar) as the signature brand color, evoking confidence and creativity.

---

## Color Philosophy

- **Primary Purple:** `#7C3AED` (vibrant, energetic, ownable)
- **Accent Gradient:** Purple to lavender for depth and visual interest
- **Background:** Clean white or very light gray for clarity and trust
- **Text:** Dark charcoal for readability; secondary text in muted gray
- **CTAs:** Bold purple buttons with white text for maximum contrast and click-through

**Reasoning:** Purple is underutilized in rewards/survey spaces, making it distinctive. It conveys creativity, confidence, and forward-thinking—qualities that resonate with women seeking flexible income.

---

## Layout Paradigm

- **Hero Section:** Bold headline + subheading + primary CTA (above fold)
- **Value Proposition:** Three pillars explaining why women should participate (Rewards, Flexibility, Community)
- **Social Proof:** Testimonials or icons showing participation/impact
- **How It Works:** Simple 3-step process (Answer → Qualify → Earn)
- **Secondary CTA:** "See if you qualify" button at bottom
- **Footer:** Minimal, trust-building (no earnings claims)

---

## Signature Elements

1. **Purple Accent Bars** — Horizontal dividers and accent lines in purple throughout
2. **Rounded Badges** — Soft, approachable badges for benefits/features
3. **Icon System** — Simple, consistent icons for each step/benefit

---

## Interaction Philosophy

- Smooth hover states on buttons (slight scale, shadow lift)
- Subtle fade-in animations for sections as user scrolls
- CTA buttons feel responsive and inviting (not aggressive)
- No countdown timers or urgency tactics (FB-compliant, trust-focused)

---

## Animation Guidelines

- **Button Hover:** Scale 1.02 + shadow increase (150ms ease-out)
- **Section Entrance:** Fade-in + slight upward slide (300ms ease-out) on scroll
- **CTA Pulse:** Gentle opacity pulse on primary button (optional, subtle)
- **Transitions:** All 200-300ms ease-out; no jarring effects

---

## Typography System

- **Display Font:** Bold, modern sans-serif (e.g., Poppins Bold or similar)
- **Body Font:** Clean, readable sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Poppins Regular)
- **Hierarchy:** 
  - H1: 42-48px, bold, dark charcoal
  - H2: 28-32px, semi-bold, dark charcoal
  - Body: 16px, regular, dark gray
  - Small: 14px, regular, muted gray

---

## Brand Essence

**One-liner:** "A rewards platform that values women's opinions and time, offering flexible ways to earn."

**Personality Adjectives:**
- Empowering
- Trustworthy
- Inclusive

**Brand Voice:**
- Headlines: Confident, direct, benefit-focused (e.g., "Your opinions are worth something" vs. "Get paid to share")
- CTAs: Inviting, low-pressure (e.g., "See if you qualify" vs. "Claim your spot now")
- Microcopy: Warm, conversational, never pushy

**Example Lines:**
- "Your voice matters. Your time is valuable."
- "Flexible rewards for women who want more."

---

## Wordmark & Logo

**Concept:** Simple, bold purple symbol (abstract upward arrow or star) + "TimeBucks" in modern sans-serif
- Logo: Standalone icon for favicon and header
- Wordmark: Icon + text lockup for hero section
- Style: Geometric, modern, instantly recognizable

---

## Signature Brand Color

**Primary Purple:** `#7C3AED`
- Used for buttons, accents, icons, and key visual elements
- Unmistakably TimeBucks across all touchpoints

---

## FB Ads Compliance Notes

- **No earnings amounts** (e.g., "$5 per survey")
- **No income guarantees** (e.g., "Earn $500/month")
- **Focus on opportunity** ("See if you qualify", "Flexible rewards")
- **Testimonials:** Only if genuine; no fabricated reviews
- **Honest copy:** "Rewards platform" not "Get rich quick"
